What this is
The bridge from "I use AI for my own stuff" to "I use AI at work, safely." Two rules carry it: use the sanctioned tool, and never paste what shouldn't leave the building.

Step 1: Find your employer's line

Before anything else, learn the policy. It decides which tools are fair game and what data is off-limits.

📋
Do this once
  • Check the employee handbook or the #ai Slack channel for an AI usage policy.
  • Note which tool is sanctioned (ChatGPT Enterprise, Claude for Work, Copilot, a Smartsheet-internal tool, etc.) — that's the one where data isn't used for training.
  • If there's no policy, ask your manager what's OK before pasting anything work-related. Asking is cheap; a leak isn't.

Step 2: Keep work data in the right bucket

🛡️
The non-negotiable
Never paste work data into a personal AI account. Sensitive-but-not-secret work data goes only in the sanctioned enterprise tool; credentials, customer PII, regulated data, and unreleased product info go in no AI tool. The full green/yellow/red system and your personal AI policy live in AI safety at work.
⭐
The decisive question
"If this conversation leaked tomorrow — a breach, a screenshot, a subpoena — would I be in trouble?" If yes: enterprise tool, or don't use AI for this one.

Step 3: Build a work setup that pays off daily

🤝
Personalize the sanctioned tool
Give it your role, your team's context, and how you like output formatted — the same 1-on-1 setup you'd do personally, inside the approved account.
⚡
Target the high-friction tasks
Status updates, meeting summaries, first-draft docs, inbox triage, reformatting. Let AI absorb the executive-function tax so your energy goes to the actual judgment calls.
✏️
A safe, high-value work prompt
Work draft (sanctioned tool)You are my writing assistant for internal status updates.
Audience: my manager + skip-level.
Tone: concise, factual, no hype.
Here are this week's notes: [PASTE — internal-but-not-secret only]
Draft a 5-bullet update. Flag anything that reads as a risk.
Keep the inputs in the yellow bucket; keep them out of your personal account.

Step 4: Become the person who "gets" AI

📈
Adopt it visibly, responsibly
  • Show, don't preach. Quietly ship better/faster work, then share the prompt when someone asks how.
  • Be the safety voice. Knowing the buckets makes you the person leadership trusts to pilot AI — that's career capital.
  • Document what works. Save winning prompts to a shared library so the whole team levels up, not just you.
☁️
Low energy takeaway
Find the sanctioned tool. Use it for one annoying recurring task this week. Never paste anything you wouldn't put on a billboard. That's the whole job.
