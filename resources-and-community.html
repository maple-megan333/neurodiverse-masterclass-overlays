About this page
Everything cited in this MasterClass lives here — peer-reviewed research, ND creators and practitioners, official tool docs, and where to find community after the course ends. Each entry tells you why it earned a spot. The list is audited quarterly for freshness and accuracy (last verified 2026-06-23; next audit ~September 2026). Pages across the course link inline to specific entries below.

1. The Research Behind This Course 11 sources

Academic and clinical papers that ground the AI + ND intersection. Cite these whenever the course makes an empirical claim about productivity gains, executive function support, or ND-specific AI use patterns.

[1]Lyu et al., "Exploring Large Language Models Through a Neurodivergent Lens," ACM CSCW, Jan 2025
Qualitative study of 61 neurodivergent Reddit communities. Identifies 20 LLM use cases across 5 themes — the framework that shapes the course's track structure.
#research#voice#communication
[2]Liao, "The Potential of Generative AI in Supporting Neurodiversity in Higher Education," ASIS&T, 2025
Systematic review of 21 studies (2022–2025). The strongest single citation for "GenAI helps ND learners" claims — writing fluency, time management, engagement.
#research#executive-function#productivity
[3]Wang & Rapp, "Navigating Neurodivergence with AI Chatbots," ACM CHI '26, April 2026NEW 2026
23 semi-structured interviews. Documents the tensions: participants found chatbot responses "robotic and inauthentic." A critical counterweight to AI-utopian framing.
#research#RSD#communication
[4]Morgan, "Reasonable Adjustments for Neurodivergent Students," Disability & Society, 2026NEW 2026
Reframes LLMs as accessibility tools that scaffold expression while preserving intellectual authorship. Grounds the "AI augments, doesn't replace" argument in UK equality law.
#research#accessibility
[5]Brewer et al., "Rethinking Productivity with GenAI: A Neurodivergent Students' Perspective," 2025
Identifies four mechanisms by which GenAI mitigates ADHD programming challenges. Documents up to 55% productivity increases — the headline stat for task decomposition claims.
#research#executive-function#productivity
[6]"Toward Neurodivergent-Aware Productivity: A Human-in-the-Loop Framework for ADHD Professionals," CHItaly '25 / arXiv, July 2025
Argues most existing tools are "behavior-focused and lack real-time adaptation." Advocates lightweight, privacy-first AI — the basis for the course's privacy stance.
#research#productivity#accessibility
[7]"Multimodal Digital Therapeutics for ADHD: Network Meta-Analysis," JMIR Preprints, March 2026PREPRINT
First rigorous RCT-aggregated evidence that AI-integrated cognitive-motor dual-task interventions work for ADHD core symptoms. Cite with "preprint" qualifier — not yet peer-reviewed.
#research#executive-function
[8]"AI for Adults with ADHD: Opportunities to Enhance Executive Functioning," Occupational Therapy Now, April 2025
Positions AI as a "potent self-management resource" for executive functioning. Adds clinical credibility distinct from CS/HCI literature — places AI inside the existing OT toolkit.
#research#executive-function
[8b]"Designing Inclusive AI Interaction for Neurodiversity," ACM CSCW '25 Companion, 2025NEW 2025
Identifies critical design opportunities for empowering ND social authorship. Useful when arguing ND-aware AI design isn't a niche concern — it's an active CSCW research thread.
#research#accessibility#communication
[8c]"Understanding Human-AI Misalignment in LLM-Based Job-Seeking Support for Neurodivergent Users," ACM ASSETS '25, 2025NEW 2025
Studies job-seeking LLM use among ADHD, autistic, dyslexic users. Documents over-formalization and neurotypical assumptions — the basis for "push back on AI's defaults" lessons.
#research#communication#productivity
[35b]CDT, "Impacts of Social Relationships with AI Chatbots on Neurodivergent Young Adults," 2026NEW 2026
Civil-liberties research initiative investigating ND young adults and AI chatbots. Signals that digital-rights orgs are taking AI+ND seriously — useful for parasocial-risk discussions.
#research#community#RSD

3. Academic & Professional 8 sources

Clinical, professional-body, and accessibility-standards sources. Cite these when the course needs institutional credibility (not just lived-experience credibility).

[18]CHADD, "Harnessing Artificial Intelligence to Live Better with ADHD," Attention Magazine
Reviews ChatGPT, Goblin Tools, Otter.ai, Claude, and wearable AI for ADHD. CHADD is the largest US ADHD advocacy org — strongest institutional credibility citation available.
#research#tool#community
[19]CHADD, "Can AI Revolutionize Education for Students with ADHD?"NEW 2026
Notes AI is "a powerful tool to complement traditional teaching methods... not a substitute for good instruction." Useful guardrail for the "AI augments, doesn't replace" framing.
#research#accessibility
[20]Edge Foundation, "Beyond Productivity Hacks: AI as Cognitive Support for ADHD Adults," 2026NEW 2026
Reframes AI from productivity hack to executive function cognitive support — "stabilize thinking, reduce overwhelm, support action." The framing this course adopts.
#research#executive-function
[21]ADDitude, "ChatGPT on ADHD: Using AI as an Executive Function Support"
Mainstream ADHD audience's introduction to AI. ADDitude is the largest US ADHD consumer publication — cite as "the conversation your audience has already started."
#tool#executive-function
[22]ADDitude, "AI Can Accurately Flag Pediatric ADHD: New Study," May 2026NEW 2026
Reports Nature Mental Health study: AI predicted childhood ADHD at age 5 with 92% accuracy. Cite cautiously — this is diagnostic AI, distinct from the assistive AI the course teaches.
#research
[23]W3C, "Cognitive Accessibility at W3C"
Top-level W3C cognitive accessibility hub. The course's design system claims should be checked against this.
#accessibility
[24]W3C, "WCAG 3 Working Draft — March 2026 Update"NEW 2026
Latest WCAG 3.0 draft (Bronze/Silver/Gold conformance, expanded cognitive accessibility). Candidate Recommendation expected Q4 2027 — reference this, not older WCAG 2.x.
#accessibility
[25]W3C COGA, "Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive and Learning Disabilities"
Practical design patterns for cognitive accessibility. The course's callouts, navigation, and "no shame language" rules all map to specific COGA patterns.
#accessibility

4. Tools & Integrations 7 sources

Primary vendor documentation. We link to canonical product pages, not review aggregators.

Magic ToDo (task breakdown with "spiciness" slider), Formalizer, Estimator, Judge, Compiler, and more. The single most important ND-specific AI tool. Free web, small one-time mobile fee.
#tool#executive-function
[27]Tiimo, official site
Visual planner built by and for neurodivergent people. Apple iPhone App of the Year 2025. 1M+ users. 2026 updates: improved AI learning, multiple to-do lists, redesigned Co-Planner.
#tool#executive-function#productivity
[28]Saner.AI, official site + blog
AI note organizer, voice capture, proactive day planner. 4.8/5 on Product Hunt. The Saner blog also publishes useful "best ADHD apps" roundups.
#tool#productivity
[29]Anthropic, Claude Cowork — general availability announcement, April 2026NEW 2026
Cowork moved from research preview (Jan 2026) to GA across all paid plans (April 2026). Grants Claude access to user-specified folders for autonomous file edits and multi-step tasks. The biggest 2026 feature drop for non-developer ND users.
#tool#productivity
[30]Anthropic, Claude product pricing and feature matrix
Canonical source for what's in Free vs Pro vs Max. Tier boundaries shifted in 2026 — Memory, Skills, Connectors, and visualizations all extended to Free tier (March 2026). Course recommendations reflect that students don't need Pro to access Projects anymore.
#tool
[31]Perplexity AI, official site + Comet / Deep Research docsUPDATED
2026 updates: Deep Research now runs on Claude Opus 4.5/4.6; Model Council (Feb 2026) for parallel multi-model queries; Computer command panel; Perplexity Health; Comet AI browser. A fundamentally different "search-first" pattern than Claude/ChatGPT.
#tool#research
[32]Microsoft, "Accessibility tools for neurodiversity"
Microsoft 365 accessibility features for ND users (Immersive Reader, Focus, Speaker Coach). Relevant for corporate ND users in the Work & Productivity track.

Honest limitations
This library is English-language and Western-skewed. Most cited research is adult-focused. The Voices section leans heavily on AuDHD creators — dyspraxia, dyscalculia, Tourette's, and OCD-only ND voices are under-represented. The library cites podcasts and Substack but only one YouTube creator; TikTok and Instagram ND creators are not represented (intentional — the course is text/screen-reader friendly first). We're expanding international and pediatric coverage in future audits.
