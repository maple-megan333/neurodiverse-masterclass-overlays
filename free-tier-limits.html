You can do almost everything in this MasterClass on free tiers. Don't pay until a free limit actually gets in your way. "Free is enough" is the default, not a compromise.

🛡️
Free ≠ private
Free and ~$20 paid consumer tiers train on your chats by default. That's a privacy setting, not a price tier — turn it off and learn the buckets in AI safety at work.

How to stretch a free tier

♻️
Make each message count
Write one good structured prompt instead of ten back-and-forths. The four-element prompt gets you further per message — which matters most when messages are capped.
🔀
Spread across tools
Hit a cap on one? Move the task to another free tier. Multi-AI routing doubles as a free-usage strategy.

When $20 is actually worth it

💡
Pay when one of these is true
  • You hit the free cap mid-task regularly and lose momentum (the ND tax of a broken flow is real).
  • You need a capability free locks out — advanced reasoning, image generation, Deep Research, big file analysis.
  • One tool has become your daily driver and the friction of caps costs more than $20 of your time/month.
Pick one tool to pay for first — usually whichever you reach for most. You don't need three subscriptions.
☁️
Low energy takeaway
Stay free until a cap annoys you twice in one week. Then upgrade the one tool you use most. Done.
