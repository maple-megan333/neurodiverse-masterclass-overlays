A few things to remember

Skip freely. If a phase isn’t for you, scroll past. If you bounce off a lesson, that’s data — it’s not the right one for now.

Energy > order. A low-energy day on Phase 1 beats a Phase 4 lesson you’ll forget. Match what you do to what you’ve got.

Your pace is the right pace. Most people do this over weeks, not days. There’s no clock.
