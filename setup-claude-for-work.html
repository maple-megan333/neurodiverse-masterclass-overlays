PERSONAL SESSION
Format: 1-on-1 session with Megan.  Time: 45 min.  What to bring: Recent work samples + a real project you're sitting on.

Why this exists

Most people set up Claude alone and end up with a generic chatbot. They type "help me write an email," get something that sounds nothing like them, and quietly stop using it. The point of doing this together is that you leave with Claude actually tuned for your work — not a demo version of it.

We do five passes: your domain, your voice, your reference materials, your shortcuts, your guardrails. Each one narrows the gap between what Claude produces by default and what you'd actually use. By the end you have a setup that fits, not a setup that almost fits.

What we'll cover

01 — Your domain

What work you actually do

The vocab, the deliverables, the people you serve. Claude needs to know you're a social worker, not a social media manager. We write that down precisely.

02 — Your voice

How you write

Formal, warm, direct, technical? We feed Claude examples of your real writing — past emails, reports, messages — so it learns to match your register, not produce a polished stranger's version.

03 — Your reference materials

What Claude should know about

Docs, style guides, org charts, past projects. We identify which ones are load-bearing and get them into Claude's context so you're not re-explaining your world every conversation.

04 — Your shortcuts

The 5–10 prompts you'll actually use every day

Saved, named, ready. No starting from a blank chat. You open Claude, pick your prompt, and it already knows what you need it to do.

05 — Your guardrails

When NOT to use Claude

Topics you handle yourself. Confidentiality lines. The cases where Claude's confident-sounding output is dangerous, not helpful. Knowing the limits is what makes the tool safe to use fast.

Before our session

Gather these five things. None of it needs to be polished — rough is fine, recent is what matters.

During the session

First 10 minutes

Talk through your work

I ask questions and take notes. No AI yet. The goal is to understand how you actually work before we touch anything.

10–30 minutes

Build your Claude Brief

We write your "this is who I am" document together — your domain, voice samples, context, guardrails. This becomes Claude's persistent instruction set.

30–45 minutes

Run your real project

We take the project you brought and work it through your setup. Adjust what isn't landing. Save your prompt library. You leave with something done, not just configured.

💡
How the Claude Brief works
It's a single document — a Notion page or a plain doc — that lives in your Claude Project. Every time you open Claude, it reads this brief and already knows who you are, how you think, and what you're trying to do. One setup, permanent context.

What you leave with

A Notion page or doc with your domain, voice, context, and guardrails. Paste it into any AI tool and you're configured in seconds.

Saved in your Claude Project. Open Claude, pick a prompt, and the heavy thinking is already done. No blank-page stall every time.

🚫 A shortlist of what NOT to use Claude for

The specific cases in your work where Claude should stay out of it — by topic, by audience, by risk level. Your guardrails, written down.
