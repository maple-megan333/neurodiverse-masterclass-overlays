How It Works

📈
Getting Started
Open any database in Notion. As you complete lessons, save prompts, and build workflows, progress fills automatically. Each database has board, gallery, and table views.
💡
No pressure
You don't need to fill everything in. Some people complete 3 lessons and build an entire system. Others work through every module. Both are valid. The databases are there when you need them.

AI Integration

🔄
AI-Connected?
If you connected your AI, try: "What lessons have I completed?" or "Add this prompt to my library." Your AI can read and write to these databases directly.
📋
How this works with Notion
This overlay shows the structure of your progress system. Your actual data lives in Notion — open the MasterClass template there to see your filled-in databases, track lessons, and save prompts. The overlay is your guide; Notion is your workspace.
🏆
What counts as done?
A lesson is "done" when you've used the thing it taught you at least once in real life. Not when you've read it. Not when you've saved the prompt. When you've actually done the thing.
