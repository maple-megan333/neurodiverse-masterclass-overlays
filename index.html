§ 02 The six chapters · self-paced · no required order

Six chapters,
read in any order.

★ start anywhere you have no homework — just the page that's open right now.
01/ 06
The foundation

Meeting yourself first.

Before the model, the mirror. A guided self-portrait in seven questions you didn't know you owed yourself — and the model can't answer for you.

~ 15 min energy start here
02/ 06
The conversation

Your first honest session.

How to ask a real question of an AI without losing your own voice in the answer. One short read. One practice prompt. Bring your own notebook.

~ 20 min energy medium
03/ 06
The craft

Prompting as a language.

A four-element framework that changes what the model gives back. Read it as grammar — not as tricks, not as hacks. Tools for thinking out loud, in writing.

~ 25 min energy medium
04/ 06
The studio

Building your workbench.

Accounts, memory, your primary AI, the connections that make the whole thing sing. Set up the studio once — use it for years.

~ 35 min energy high
05/ 06
The life

AI in the ordinary day.

Templates for the parts of life nobody teaches: the email, the doctor's letter, the apology you've been writing in your head for a week, the to-do that won't move.

~ 30 min energy medium
06/ 06
The recovery

When you crash, gently.

A field kit for the days the wheels come off. Read once now and bookmark it — save it for the hard week. You won't always need it. You will need it sometimes.

~ 12 min energy keep handy
